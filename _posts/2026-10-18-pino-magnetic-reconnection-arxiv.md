I'm happy to announce that my paper is now up on arXiv! ([arXiv:2609.29514](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29514)). In this paper, we explore if it is possible to learn magnetic reconnection in 2D using a Physics Informed Neural Operator (PINO). We set up the 2D problem and develop our PINO with important physics residuals, and explore the operator results. We achieve a significant speedup over traditional solvers with a very marginal error, and since this is an operator model, we can scale to higher resolutions directly!

Read the paper at: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29514](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29514)
